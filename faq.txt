20 QUESTIONS OUR CUSTOMERS ASK
===========================================

1. What exactly do I get?
Three things. A short booking form. A follow-up board for pending fees. A morning plan for the day's visits.
All built for you, in your name, ready to use.

2. Is this an app my patients must download?
No. Your patient fills a short form on a link.
You send it on WhatsApp. That is all they see.

3. What does the booking form do?
It takes the basic details. Name, area, condition, preferred time.
Package enquiries get sorted for a call back, so you are not typing prices at midnight.

4. What does the follow-up board do?
It shows every patient and every rupee still pending.
Green means paid. Amber means due this week. Red means overdue.
You open it once in the morning and know where you stand.

5. What is the morning plan?
One screen that tells you the day's visits in order.
Who, where, what time, and how much is still to collect from that home.
No more scrolling WhatsApp to rebuild your day.

6. Will my patients need to pay you anything?
No. Your patient pays you. We never touch their money and never message them for payment.

7. How much does it cost?
Rs 15,000 setup, then Rs 5,000 a month.
That is it. No hidden charges.

8. What does the Rs 15,000 setup cover?
The booking form, the follow-up board, the morning plan, and the first setting up of your patient list.
We build it, test it, and hand it over working.

9. What does the Rs 5,000 a month cover?
Hosting, fixes, small changes, and support on WhatsApp.
If you add a new package or change your fees, tell us and we update it.

10. Is there a long contract?
No. Monthly. You can stop after any month.
We ask for 30 days' notice so we can hand over your data neatly.

11. How long does it take to set up?
Five working days from the day we get your details.
Most clinics are live in under a week.

12. What do you need from me to start?
Your patient list, if you have one. Your package names and prices. Your usual visit areas.
A 30 minute call with us. That is all.

13. Do I need to be good with computers?
No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.
We set it up. We sit with you once and show you the three screens.

14. What if my patient does not fill the form?
We keep a quick entry screen for you.
You type the name and time in ten seconds. The rest works the same way.

15. Can it handle my packages, like 10 sessions or a monthly plan?
Yes. You tell us your packages and prices once.
The form sorts package enquiries for a call back, so you quote the right price every time.

16. Will it remind patients about the next visit?
It gives you a ready list of who needs a confirmation message today.
You send it from your own WhatsApp, in your own words.

17. Will it get me new patients?
It will get you feedback you can ask for and share.
Happy patients get a simple request for a review or a referral.
That is how new patients start coming in.

18. What happens after I pay the setup fee?
We book your 30 minute call within one working day.
Then we build for five days. Then we hand over and train you.
You start using it the same week.

19. What if something breaks or I want a change?
Message us on WhatsApp. We reply the same working day.
Small changes are included in the monthly fee.

20. What if it does not work for my practice?
Use it for the first month. If it saves you less than it costs, tell us.
We will fix it or stop it. No argument.
